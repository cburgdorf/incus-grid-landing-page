Our Story

Born from a close call.

In October 2024, our family was sailing the Ionian Sea when a lightning strike hit right next to our boat. That terrifying moment sparked a question: Are we safer here or on the other side of the bay?

We built IncusGrid to answer that question by combining historical data and terrain analysis to help sailors find safer anchorages.
