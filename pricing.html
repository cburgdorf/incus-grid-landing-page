Questions

How does the free trial work? +

Choose the monthly or yearly plan and enter your payment details. The first 7 days are free. After that, the plan’s normal price is charged unless you cancel before. The free trial is available once per account.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. You cancel in the app under Billing. You keep full access until the end of the free trial or the period you have paid for.

What is Lifetime? +

A single payment for permanent full access, including features we add later. There is no subscription. If you already have one, it ends automatically. Lifetime is only offered for a limited time.

What stays free? +

Everything in the Free column, with no time limit and no payment details. You only need a free account, created in a minute.

What does the passage planner do without Pro? +

It plans the whole passage through the forecast wind: route, arrival, strongest wind, gusts and waves, time sailing and under motor, and the first leg hour by hour, for departures within the next 48 hours. Pro compares 12 departures and marks the best one, plans up to 10 days ahead, shows the whole route hour by hour and sails with your own polar.

Which currency do I pay in? +

Prices are shown in euros in Europe and in US dollars elsewhere. Payment is handled by Stripe, which shows the final amount before you pay.

Caution

IncusGrid is made for planning and is not a navigation aid. Routes are drawn schematically, and berths are derived from stays in boat reports; they are not confirmed or bookable places. The passage planner uses a simple polar, without currents or the effect of waves on boat speed, and does not route you into the harbour. Always check current forecasts, official charts and local conditions before you go to sea.
