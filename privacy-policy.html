3. Data We Collect

When you use IncusGrid on the web or in our Android and iOS apps, we may collect:

  • Account information: your email address and, if you sign in with Apple or Google, the name and email address the provider shares
  • Access and billing status of your account; payments are handled by Stripe, and we never receive your card details
  • Usage data: map interactions and feature usage, measured with Google Analytics (on the website only with your consent, see section 9)
  • Technical data: browser or app version, device type

The location button on the map uses your device's position only on your device to show it on the map. We do not receive or store your location.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide your account, sign-in and access
  • Send you emails about your account and, unless you opt out, about IncusGrid
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve the service
  • Ensure the security of our platform

We do not sell your data and do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

5. Service Providers

We use these providers, each only for the purpose named:

  • Google Analytics (Google): usage statistics (on the website with your consent)
  • Stripe: payments
  • Brevo: sending emails
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, if you choose it
  • MapTiler: map tiles and place search
  • Cloudflare: delivery of map data
  • Google Ads (Google) and Meta: measuring our advertising on the website, with your consent (not in the apps)

6. Data Storage, Retention and Deletion

Your account data is stored on our own server and protected by appropriate technical and organizational measures. We keep it as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the account menu of the website or the apps, or by writing to hello@incusgrid.com. Deleting the account removes your account data, ends a running subscription and removes your email address from our mailing provider. Payment records are kept by Stripe and as required by tax and accounting law.

9. Analytics and Advertising

We use the services below to understand how IncusGrid is used and to measure whether our ads lead to sign-ups. They set cookies or similar identifiers on your device and are only active with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time in the Cookie Settings, with effect for the future.

Google Analytics

Our website, blog, web application and our Android and iOS apps use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google"). Google Analytics records which pages you view and what you do in the application (for example opening a map layer, signing up or starting a checkout), the website that referred you, campaign parameters in the link, device and browser type, and your approximate location. Google Analytics does not store your full IP address. Cookies used: _ga, _ga_*. Event data is deleted after 2 months, data linked to a user identifier after 14 months of inactivity. In the Android and iOS apps, Google Analytics measures usage only: Google signals and advertising features are switched off there, and the apps do not use the Meta Pixel.

Google signals. On the website: if you are signed in to a Google account and have allowed ads personalisation there, Google links the data from our website with your Google account. We only receive aggregated reports from this (for example how many visitors use more than one device); it does not identify you to us. You can manage this in your Google My Ad Center and delete the data in Google My Activity.

Google Ads conversion measurement. Our Google Analytics property is linked to our Google Ads account. If you reach us by clicking one of our Google or YouTube ads, the click identifier from the link is stored in a cookie (_gcl_*), and if you then sign up, Google Analytics reports this sign-up to Google Ads. We use this to see which ads lead to sign-ups and to let Google show our ads to people more likely to be interested. Your email address and account details are not passed to Google.

Meta Pixel

Our web application uses the Meta Pixel, provided by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, D04 X2K5, Ireland ("Meta"), to measure our ads on Instagram and Facebook. The pixel sends Meta a page view when you open the application and an event when you sign up, start a free trial or complete a purchase (with the amount and currency, but without your email address or payment details). Meta can link this with your Instagram or Facebook account if you are logged in. Cookies used: _fbp, _fbc. For the collection and transmission of this data we and Meta are joint controllers (Art. 26 GDPR) under Meta's Controller Addendum; Meta is responsible for any further processing, described in the Meta Privacy Policy. You can manage ads on Meta in your ad preferences. The pixel is not used in our Android and iOS apps.

Transfers outside the EU

Google and Meta may process data in the United States. Both companies are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, on which the European Commission's adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 is based (Art. 45 GDPR).

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us at hello@incusgrid.com.
